For council and housing-association tenants in England

Mould in your home? Your landlord is now on a legal clock.

Awaab's Law gives social landlords fixed deadlines to investigate and fix damp and mould that puts your health at risk. Show Mouldproof your photos and tell it what happened. It works out where your landlord stands against those deadlines, checks the landlord's record, and writes the letters that get things moving.
